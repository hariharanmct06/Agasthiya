Correspondence System · Prof. Park Jae Woo

Sujok Healing

A profound correspondence system viewing the hands and feet as complete holographic reflections of the entire human body.

Traditional Context

Developed by South Korean scientist and philosopher Professor Park Jae Woo. In Korean, ‘Su’ signifies hand and ‘Jok’ signifies foot. The system is based on the traditional understanding that human hands and feet mirror the anatomy and energy meridian networks of the physical body.

What to Ask Before Your Session

You can ask which areas of your hands correspond to your personal areas of tension, what simple home touch stimulation you can practice between sessions, and how colour or seeds can be used to harmonize correspondence zones.

Comfort & Consent

Completely non-invasive. You remain fully clothed, seated comfortably in a relaxed posture. Hariharan works exclusively with gentle palpation probes, finger touch, or color markers on the hands. Every step is explained beforehand.

At a Glance

Key elements of this session:

  • Non-invasive hand/foot correspondence
  • Gentle pressure, seeds, or color markers
  • Personal self-care guidance included

Japanese Natural Healing · Mikao Usui

Usui Reiki

A deeply calming Japanese spiritual practice channelling universal life force energy to encourage emotional quietude and physical ease.

Traditional Context

Founded in Japan by Mikao Usui in the early 20th century. ‘Rei’ represents universal spiritual wisdom, and ‘Ki’ represents vital life energy. In the traditional Usui framework, the practitioner serves as a clear conduit through which calming vitality flows.

What to Ask Before Your Session

Ask about whether you prefer gentle stationary touch or a completely hands-off session (hands hovering 2–4 inches above the body), how people typically experience sensations like mild warmth or tingles, and how to relax during the flow.

Comfort & Consent

Conducted fully clothed resting on a comfortable treatment couch or seated chair. Your boundary preferences are agreed upon before the session begins. You can request changes or pause at any moment.

At a Glance

Key elements of this session:

  • Gentle non-invasive touch or hands-off
  • Deep nervous system relaxation
  • Emotional centering and stress release

Sacred Buddhist Healing Tradition · Sadguru Satyanandaji

Lama Fera

A powerful, traditional Buddhist energy technique utilized for clearing heavy energetic blockages and fostering deep spiritual equilibrium.

Traditional Context

Originating in the Himalayan Buddhist monasteries and adapted into a structured discipline by Sadguru Satyanandaji. The practice invokes Lord Buddha’s compassionate consciousness using traditional sacred symbols, crystal wands, and rhythmic mantras to release deep emotional heaviness.

What to Ask Before Your Session

Ask about the sacred symbols utilized during the clearing, the non-touch nature of the ritual, and what grounding recommendations are suggested after an energy-clearing session.

Comfort & Consent

Strictly a non-touch modality. The client sits or lies down quietly while the practitioner works around the subtle energy field (aura). Respectful silence and comfortable surroundings are maintained throughout.

At a Glance

Key elements of this session:

  • 100% non-touch aura harmonization
  • Clearing emotional exhaustion & heaviness
  • Sacred Buddhist symbols and grounding

Compassion & Grace · Bodhisattva Kwan Yin

Kwan Yin Healing

A tender, heart-centered healing practice invoking the compassionate presence of Bodhisattva Kwan Yin to soften emotional distress and nurture self-kindness.

Traditional Context

Inspired by Bodhisattva Kwan Yin (Guanyin), revered throughout Eastern spiritual traditions as the personification of boundless mercy, compassion, and unconditional acceptance. The practice focuses on opening the heart chakra and transmuting grief, resentment, or inner harshness into peace.

What to Ask Before Your Session

Ask how compassion-based energy work can support personal emotional processing, release longstanding feelings of guilt or grief, and cultivate gentle self-acceptance.

Comfort & Consent

A tranquil, deeply comforting experience. Sessions are non-invasive and non-touch, conducted with meditative music, gentle breathing guidance, and respectful emotional boundaries.

At a Glance

Key elements of this session:

  • Heart-centered compassionate energy
  • Emotional grief & forgiveness release
  • Quiet meditation and inner stillness

Amazonian Shamanic Tradition · Guaraní People

Ama Deus Healing

A sacred shamanic energy healing tradition connecting the human heart with the loving vitality of nature and universal divine love.

Traditional Context

Preserved for generations by the Guaraní shamans living in the Amazon rainforest of South America and brought to modern awareness by Alberto Aguas. The tradition uses the breath, sacred spiritual symbols, and intention to channel unconditional divine love for physical and spiritual balance.

What to Ask Before Your Session

Ask about the role of the spiritual heart center, how breathwork integrates with ancient Amazonian symbols, and how this modality connects with the vitality of the natural world.

Comfort & Consent

Client remains fully clothed in a restful posture. The session utilizes breath and energy visualization, with optional light hand placement over the heart or solar plexus only after your explicit prior agreement.

At a Glance

Key elements of this session:

  • Ancient Guaraní shamanic tradition
  • Connection to nature's living energy
  • Gentle breath and heart harmonization

Hariharan’s Particular Strength

A thoughtful approach to colour.
A personal approach to wellbeing.

“Colour is not merely decorative; in traditional Eastern wisdom, each color carries a distinct vibrational quality that can encourage balance where there is excess or depletion.”

Within the Sujok healing discipline, colour therapy is my core area of focus and dedication. Developed by South Korean scientist Professor Park Jae Woo, Sujok reveals how the entire human organism is mirrored with remarkable precision on the hands and feet.

In traditional Sujok philosophy, health is an equilibrium among the Five Elements and Six Ki energies (Wind, Heat, Hotness, Humidity, Dryness, and Cold). Rather than using invasive needles or forceful manipulation, Sujok colour therapy applies specific visible colors directly onto corresponding zones or Byol energy points.

This gentle method is safe, non-toxic, and entirely non-invasive—making it accessible for individuals of all ages.

Questions to Discuss Before Your Session:

  • How do specific hand correspondence zones relate to my day-to-day comfort?
  • Which elemental color frequencies correspond with my constitutional balance?
  • How can I continue gentle color or seed stimulation on my hands between visits?
